TRUE ITALY
GRAND TOUR

In 2027, TRUE introduces a new way to experience Italy.

MORE THAN 180 OF THE WORLD'S LEADING LUXURY TRAVEL ADVISORS, MEDIA AND PARTNERS WILL TRAVEL ACROSS THE COUNTRY THROUGH THREE CONNECTED GRAND TOURS, EACH DESIGNED AROUND TWO COMPLEMENTARY ITALIAN DESTINATIONS.

BUYERS WILL BE DIVIDED INTO THREE MAIN GROUPS AND WILL EXPERIENCE TWO REGIONS AT A TIME THROUGH A COMBINATION OF DEDICATED PRE-FAM TRIPS, IMMERSIVE MAIN EVENTS AND POST-FAM JOURNEYS.

Private Air Transfers
✈

Each transition between destinations happens aboard a private SKYALPS jet, exclusively organized by TRUE - transforming the movement itself into part of the experience.
