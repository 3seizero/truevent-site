It was presented at TRUE Sicily 2026, the luxury travel trade event held at Verdura Resort from November 10 to 13, 2026.

01Sicily's tourism growth, 2024-2026

Sicily entered 2026 from an already high base. In 2024 the regional series, including short-term rentals, recorded more than 22.4 million overnight stays. International travellers accounted for 12.05 million of them.

Growth continued through 2025 and accelerated again in 2026. From January to August 2026, arrivals rose 3.6% and overnight stays 5.8% compared with the same period of 2025.

On a comparable January-August basis, the compounded two-year change is about +18.4% in overnight stays and +4.9% in arrivals. Nights have grown around four times faster than arrivals: travellers are staying longer.

What it means for luxury travel

More nights per trip mean more room nights and more spending on restaurants, private transfers, guides, experiences, wine, shopping and additional destinations on the island.

SourcesRegione Siciliana / Osservatorio Turistico; Regional Tourism Department, 2026; UniCredit / Prometeia, 2026.

International travel has been one of the strongest drivers of Sicily's growth. Foreign travellers already generated 12.05 million nights in 2024, and by 2025 international demand had strengthened further while domestic demand softened.

In the first half of 2026, North American arrivals rose 8.5% and French demand 4.2%. A larger international base supports five-star hotels, luxury resorts, villas, DMC services, private transfers and a broader shoulder season.

American overnight stays in the province reached 103,227 in 2025, with an average stay of 3.8 nights. The area brings together Verdura, Sciacca, Agrigento, Selinunte and Menfi, in a market where international travellers already generate half of all nights.

The luxury opportunity is measurable: established resorts, major archaeology, wine and coastline can support longer stays and higher-value itineraries, while the area is still less internationally developed than Taormina or Palermo.

January and March 2026 each grew by about 7%, while overnight stays from January to August rose 5.8%. Summer demand remained high. The opportunity is to grow April to June and September to November through culture, wine, gastronomy, archaeology and golf.

Longer stays and stronger demand for experiences increase the value of private guides, restaurants, wineries, boats, drivers, artisans and cultural access. Growth in nights and experiences matters more economically than growth in arrivals alone.

10East and West: two complementary growth stories

Eastern and Western Sicily are not competing destinations. Their different levels of international recognition, hotel infrastructure and visitor distribution can help sell richer, longer itineraries across the whole island.

Eastern Sicily: the established luxury benchmark

From Taormina and Mount Etna to Catania, Siracusa and the Val di Noto, Eastern Sicily combines international brand recognition, iconic cultural sites and a mature luxury hospitality offer.

3.99M

Taormina remains Sicily's most internationally established luxury destination. Heritage hotels, international hospitality brands, dramatic scenery and cultural programming have supported premium pricing, with leading five-star average daily rates approaching €1,500. These figures describe the top of the Taormina market, not an island-wide average.

Catania and Etna

Airport access, the volcanic landscape, wineries and outdoor experiences make a gateway for premium itineraries and shoulder-season demand.

Siracusa and Ortigia

Archaeology, architecture, waterfront hospitality and private cultural experiences appeal strongly to experiential luxury travellers.

Val di Noto

Noto, Modica and Ragusa bring UNESCO-listed Baroque heritage, design-led stays and a distinctive food identity.

What comes next: Eastern Sicily's opportunity is to build on established demand while spreading tourism more widely across the region. Better connections between the major gateways, resorts and cultural destinations can encourage longer itineraries and reduce concentration in peak-season hotspots.

Western Sicily: a growing luxury destination

Western and Southwestern Sicily bring together Agrigento, Sciacca, Menfi, Selinunte, Marsala, Trapani and Palermo. The area offers a complementary luxury proposition built on coastal resorts, archaeology, wineries and food, connected in an itinerary rather than centred on one resort town.

Sciacca recorded 514,897 overnight stays in 2025. The Agrigento-Sciacca corridor links the Valley of the Temples with resort hospitality and the surrounding coastline, and US nights in the province rose 15.9% in 2025.

Rocco Forte's Verdura Resort is an internationally recognised luxury anchor. Its golf, wellness, food and coastal setting support premium stays, and its location introduces guests to Agrigento, Selinunte and Menfi. The opportunity is to connect international resort demand with local guides, wineries, restaurants, transport and cultural experiences.

Selinunte and Menfi

Archaeology, vineyards and coastal experiences can extend resort-based stays.

Marsala and Trapani

Wine heritage, historic towns, the salt pans and access to the Egadi Islands support varied itineraries.

Palermo

A major international city gateway, cultural destination and complementary stop on a western itinerary.

The development priority: the opportunity is to turn strong cultural interest and growing international demand into longer stays, higher local spending and more year-round employment. Destination access, consistent service quality and high-end experiences will be essential.

Distinct commercial advantages

Western Sicily

Established luxury anchors alongside emerging circuits, strong archaeological and coastal assets, meaningful international demand and opportunities to build higher-value multi-stop itineraries.

11The numbers, and what they mean for the trade

What it means for advisors and hoteliers

  1. Sell more nights, not only more arrivals. Nights are growing much faster than arrivals, and Sicily has the product for longer stays across several destinations.
  2. Build for international demand. Foreign travellers are the majority, and increasingly important outside the summer peak.
  3. Treat North America as a core luxury market. Almost one million American nights in 2024, and North American demand is still growing.
  4. Follow the rate growth. Luxury rates are 43% above 2019, and Taormina shows the spending power at the top of the market.
  5. Watch Southwestern Sicily. About half of Agrigento's nights are international, and American nights are growing at double-digit rates.
  6. Sell the shoulder season. Culture, wine, food and golf give Sicily the product to grow beyond July and August.

FAQFrequently asked questions

About Sicily

Is Sicily a luxury travel destination?
Yes. Taormina's leading five-star hotels reach average daily rates of about €1,500, luxury hotel rates across Sicily are 43% above 2019, and more than 2,500 premium hotel rooms are in the pipeline to 2030. Palermo, Siracusa, the Val di Noto and the southwest around Verdura Resort complete the offer.
Is tourism in Sicily growing?
Yes. From January to August 2026, overnight stays rose 5.8% and arrivals 3.6% compared with 2025, about +18.4% in nights compared with 2024. International travellers account for more than half of all nights.
How important are American travellers to Sicily?
Very. The United States was Sicily's second-largest foreign market in 2024, with about 380,000 arrivals and almost one million nights. North American arrivals rose a further 8.5% in the first half of 2026.
When is the best time for luxury travel to Sicily?
April to June and September to November combine pleasant weather with culture, wine, food, archaeology and golf, and fewer crowds than July and August. January and March 2026 each grew by about 7%, a sign that the season is getting longer.
