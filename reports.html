Sardinia 2026: The Numbers Behind Italy's Fastest-Growing Luxury Destination

In 2023, Sardinia was the only Italian region where tourism declined. In 2025, ISTAT ranked it first in Italy for tourism growth. This report explains the turnaround with official data: who is travelling to Sardinia, where on the island the growth is happening, and what the 2026 season shows about the future of luxury travel in Sardinia.
